## Notes
- Screenshot titles are the sites' brand names (ATELO, Zuno, CTGT, Nexora, Juris...) but no URLs are stored; several look like template/concept shots (Dribbble/Framer-style), so treat provenance as unknown.
- Styles with warnings (Warm Editorial, Bento Grid) are labelled neutral here: the repo calls them legitimate but "dangerous reflex"/"becoming a default".
- Spot-checks: grillitype.com live; helveticanow.com 302 -> linotype.com; ndc.co.jp/hara/en/ fetch failed. "y2k.institute" label points to cameronsworld.net.
- Repo has no hosted demo; its gallery app runs locally via `python server.py`. YouTube companion video (README.md:5) excluded as not a design artifact.
- DNA never-list (purple gradients, 3D SaaS blobs, icon-grid features, Inter-only, rounded-everything) is a useful slop rubric, not an example set.
