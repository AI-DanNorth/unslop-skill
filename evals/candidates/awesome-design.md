## Best audit candidates

1. https://www.ibm.com/design/language/ — rigorous "good" baseline for grid, type scale, colour tokens.
2. https://atlassian.design/ — full product design system; component-level good baseline.
3. http://www.bbc.co.uk/gel — accessibility-first public system; tests a11y rules.
4. https://lineto.com/ — restrained type-led studio site; anti-template "good" pole.
5. https://www.siteinspire.com/ — live, curated; harvest crafted sites as good examples.
6. https://land-book.com/ — mixed pool; sample both crafted and template SaaS landings.
7. https://www.froala.com/design-blocks — live generic Bootstrap blocks; near-perfect "template slop" reference.
8. https://dribbble.com/ — concept shots as the canonical slop aesthetic (gradients, fake metrics, glass).
9. https://uigradients.com/ — the default-gradient vector behind AI hero sections.
10. http://nicelydone.club/ — real product flows; tests UX-level (not just visual) rules.

## Notes

- List is old (most entries 2016–2019); expect dead links (Starbucks, Rizzo, Zurb PatternTap, Prott, Haiku, Atomic, Validately). Spot-checked 5: Siteinspire, Froala, Klim, Lapa live; Land-book fetch failed (client error, likely bot-blocking).
- Excluded as non-artifacts: books (Amazon), podcasts, conferences, tutorials, stock/icon/font libraries, user-testing SaaS.
- Duplicate entry: Atlassian listed twice (atlassian.design / www.atlassian.design); counted once.
- "slop" labels mark sources of default patterns (templates, gradient presets, concept shots), not AI-generated pages.
- Galleries are pools: audit individual sites drawn from them rather than the gallery chrome.
