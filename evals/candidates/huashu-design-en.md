## Best audit candidates
1. scripts/tests/fixtures/lint_slop.html (#53): ground-truth 2026 AI-default page; pair with lint_reskin (#54) and lint_clean (#55) as a labeled slop / reskin / clean triad.
2. assets/showcases/website-saas/saas-{pentagram,build,takram}.html (#42): one brief in 3 styles. The Build variant (floating cards, warm-gold gradient) is a good borderline case.
3. assets/showcases/ppt/ppt-*.html (#37): 1920×1080 data slides, 3 variants. Decks are scarce elsewhere.
4. assets/showcases/infographic/infographic-*.html (#38): vertical 1080×1920 infographics, 3 variants.
5. assets/style-specimens/xhs/*.html + scripts/tests/fixtures/xhs_bad.html / xhs_ok.html (#49, #60, #61): social carousel cards with good and bad labels. Very rare elsewhere.
6. https://skill-huasheng.vercel.app (#2): live 13-slide HTML keynote with embedded cinematic demos.
7. https://www.huasheng.ai/huashu-design-hero/ + hero-animation-v10-en.mp4 (#3, #4): real motion and video artifact.
8. https://www.huasheng.ai/parrots/ (#1): live AI-made editorial site with real imagery. Good test for whether AI output that avoids slop passes.
9. assets/style-specimens/web/13-glassmorphism-bento.html + ppt/09-bento-grid.html (#50, #51): legitimate versions of flagged families, useful as a false-positive test.
10. scripts/tests/fixtures/lint_tricky_bad.html / lint_tricky_ok.html (#57, #58): readability and contrast edge cases (ghost text, a dark plate under text).

## Notes
- Almost every artifact in the repo was made by AI with this skill and is presented as good. Treat them as candidates that "claim to be unslopped", not as human-designed ground truth.
- The only explicit slop artifacts are the lint fixtures (#53, #54, #56, #57, #60). The repo's own design_lint.py fixtures are ready-made calibration labels.
- Rows #44–49 are grouped sets totaling 92 specimen HTML+JPG pairs; the sheet-*.jpg contact sheets show each section on one image.
- Taxonomy nuance: the repo treats default-look families 1–4 as "look families" (notices, legitimate when chosen) and template chrome (5) as yellow lights (content-guidelines.md:17-20). Unslop should weigh them differently.
- Most on-page copy is Chinese, which makes these useful for CJK typography checks. Only #1–3 were spot-checked live; every row marked "URL inferred" is unverified.
