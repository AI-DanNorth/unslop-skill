## Notes

- Repo is a commercial motion library; all its own pages are self-labeled good. Pro pages show previews but gated code.
- The repo's machine-readable motion rules live in agent/lib/rules.mjs (transition-all, slow-duration, no-reduced-motion, untransitioned-overlay, inconsistent-durations) and recipe-specs.json; useful for calibrating a motion lens.
- Generic-vs-skill pages switch via ?style=generic; the slop is synthetic (authored to mimic AI defaults), not captured from a real AI run.
- Liveness spot-checked (5): transitions.dev, example.html?style=generic, transitions/menu-dropdown/, animations.dev, invisibledetails.com all live.
- Recipes 33-35 (text morph, soft text swap, donut chart) exist only as skill files, with no sitemap page.
