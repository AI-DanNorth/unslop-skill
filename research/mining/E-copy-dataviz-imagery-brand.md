## Conflicts
- Em dashes: hallmark, kami, agsk ban them (0); taste allows 2 in body (WARN above) with a stricter zero alternative. Stricter value adopted in CPY-138; taste budget kept as a waiver level.
- Quotes: hallmark and agsk require curly quotes; kami §235 prefers straight quotes unless the doc already uses typographic quotes.
- Arrows: kami §233 says use `->` rather than `→` in documents; hallmark/huashu ban any arrow glued to a link/button label. Not a direct clash, but `->` in prose is still disputed.
- Numerals: agsk "8 deployments" (numerals for counts) vs taste "eleven people came" (spelled odd human numbers in marketing copy).
- h1 length: hallmark ≤7 words/~50 chars vs taste 2-8 words (prof. heroes 2-9). Page word totals: hallmark ~450 vs taste ~900; words per screen hallmark ~80 (fail >95) vs taste 34-47 by kind, 60-80 tech.
- Sub-line length: hallmark ≤12 words vs taste 15-30 words for software.
- Hero actions: taste allows 2 hero actions; hallmark bans a stock second action and prefers one; kami allows one secondary + one primary.
- Pie/donut: hallmark never; kami donut ≤6 segments; uupm pie/donut ≤5 categories with labels.
- Red/green: kami never; uupm uses red/green with signed values and icons; scientific style uses blue/orange.
- Chart titles: kami §429 says the chart draws no title (caption carries takeaway); others put an insight headline on the chart. Both agree the takeaway must be stated once.
- Diagram captions: kami requires a one-line insight caption; taste says no caption under a diagram (card title says what it shows).
- Photo treatment: hallmark applies the theme treatment (warm, mono, duotone, tint) to every photo; taste bans any filter, duotone, grain or blend on page photographs.
- Text on photos: hallmark allows a 35-65% theme-ink scrim; taste bans scrims/gradients/blur and never puts text on place photos.
- Photo credits: hallmark lists author, licence and link in one footer line; taste names sources only (≤110 chars) linking to a credits page; public-domain still names photographer.
- Sample data: hallmark recommends `example.com` and "Example Kitchen" inside built product samples; taste bans printed example.com/.example addresses and "sample" tags.
- Honest placeholder: huashu prefers a grey block labelled "illustration placeholder 1200×800" over drawn SVG; hallmark/taste ban visible placeholder labels on finished pages (slot must show something). Resolve by surface: drafts vs ship.
- Icon stroke: taste Lucide stroke 2; uupm brand standard 1.5px; kami diagram icons 1-1.5.
- Node budget: kami ≤9 nodes per figure; taste ≤12 nodes per diagram card.
- Slide copywriting formulas in uupm §291 ("[Number]+ companies trust", "[Old way] is dead. Meet [new way].", gradient title text) conflict with hallmark/taste bans on invented metrics and template headlines; treat uupm formulas as structure only.
- Brand colour psychology palettes (uupm §256 "tech: #6366F1/#8B5CF6") conflict with taste's violet/lavender ban for a page's own palette and hallmark's "framework default is not a brand".
