# Glossary

**Artifact**: the thing being audited (a page, deck, document, screen set, card, email or film).

**Contract**: `00-contract.md`, the intake's record of what the artifact is, who it is for and what authority it answers to.

**Visitor mode**: what the surface is for. *Persuade* (marketing, sales), *Operate* (product UI, tools), *Read* (docs, articles, reports) or *Experience* (portfolio, campaign, film). Rules and weights vary by mode.

**Floor / Reflex / Finish**: the three tiers (severity.md).

**Look family**: a cluster of defaults that makes AI-made work recognisable, defined numerically in `data/look-families.yaml`: warm-paper serif, dark single-accent, hairline newspaper, SaaS card kit, violet gradient. One tell alone is info; three or more clustered on one surface is a finding.

**Tell**: a single detectable default (an eyebrow, a gradient pair, a stock phrase).

**Fingerprint**: six structural axes recorded per delivery: heading placement, body composition, divider language, button voice, image treatment, reveal pattern.

**Ledger**: the record of past deliveries' fingerprints and palettes, used to catch repetition across outputs.

**Focal point**: the one element a screen, slide or frame is built around.

**Fold widths**: 1280×800, 1440×900 and 1920×1080, the viewports where hero geometry is judged.

**Control**: a known-good file rendered before every capture to prove the renderer works.

**Seed**: a planted defect used to prove a detector catches what it claims to.
